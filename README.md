### Open research and projects

**[AI Search Visibility in Italia — 2026](https://github.com/gentian-hajdaraj/ai-search-visibility-italia)**  
A research companion repository for a structured study of **6,000 observations** across **ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode, and Google AI Overview**. Includes a documented query panel, methodology, metric definitions, published aggregates, and citation metadata.

- [Research report](https://telescop.it/research/ai-search-visibility-italia/)
- [Dataset and methodology (Zenodo DOI)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22640375)
- [Citation guidance](https://github.com/gentian-hajdaraj/ai-search-visibility-italia/blob/main/CITING.md)

**[Visibility Benchmark Lite — public tool](https://github.com/gentian-hajdaraj/ai-search-visibility-italia/tree/main/tools/visibility-benchmark-lite)**  
A small, browser-only tool for exploring **user-annotated** AI search observations with explicit denominators and separate measures for mentions, recommendations, and citations. Available as an open-source research companion; includes synthetic example data, not the published study data.

### Research approach

I care about the differences between **mention vs. recommendation vs. citation**, **an observed absence vs. missing evidence**, and **a repeatable method vs. an unsupported claim**. Public projects include methodological limits and documented source provenance wherever possible.
